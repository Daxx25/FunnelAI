🚀 FunnelAI — AI-Powered Sales Pipeline Intelligence

FunnelAI is an AI-driven revenue analytics platform that transforms raw CRM data into actionable business insights. It automates pipeline analysis, tracks conversion efficiency, and surfaces key revenue drivers to help sales and operations teams make faster, data-informed decisions.

💡 Overview

Modern revenue teams rely heavily on CRM systems, but extracting meaningful insights often requires manual effort and fragmented reporting.

FunnelAI solves this by:

ingesting raw CRM exports
standardizing and structuring messy data
generating real-time dashboards
delivering AI-powered insights on pipeline health
✨ Key Capabilities
📊 Automated Pipeline Analytics
Full funnel visibility from Lead → Closed Won/Lost
Stage-wise conversion tracking
Drop-off detection across pipeline
📈 Revenue Intelligence
Revenue trends over time
Performance by sales representative
Identification of high-impact revenue drivers
🤖 AI-Generated Insights
Detects bottlenecks and anomalies

🧱 System Architecture

FunnelAI is built around a modular analytics pipeline:

Data Ingestion
Accepts structured CRM datasets
Data Processing & Cleaning
Normalizes pipeline stages
Handles missing or inconsistent values
Parses and aligns temporal data
Metrics Engine
Pipeline funnel computation
Conversion rate analysis
Revenue aggregation and segmentation
Visualization Layer
Interactive dashboards for exploration
Funnel, trend, and performance views
AI Insight Engine
Translates metrics into business insights
Focuses on trends, bottlenecks, and recommendations
